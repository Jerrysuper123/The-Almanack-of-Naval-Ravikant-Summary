# Think clearly about yourself, your life and environment (principles)

`disaster come when people praise you so highly, so you must know who you really are as a person`

`the failures that other people made; it is likely you will make also. Do not be fooling yourself that you would not.`

Success does not really reflect a person. Look at their failure experiences - opposite of the society's view. When you read more of their failures, you will instinctively reflect that shit, i might be commiting this mistake also - no good self control, no discipline, no persistence

`who you are, what do you want to do, and what needed to be given up`

`society likes to paint beautifully someone's success, but what really defines a person is the failures or setbacks`

We only build success step by step and brick by brick. There is no fast lane, and I tried it and could not do it. It is best to throw any MVP into the market, and accept the failures openly and improve it. Failures help us to improve; we need these bricks in our life journey. This journey requires high persistence.

`money all do not matter; ultimately is the family you created memory with`

`it is not about whether you have desires for money; it is about how strong you want to solve a problem to create value for the society`

Start a business only when you are compelled with strong motivation to create value. It starts with your own problems that you want to resolve - it is more personal, more motivation

`Excellency in product or people is only nurtured in tough environment`

So love the tough environment

`small success might come from yourself. Big sucess surely needs to come from a group of people. Huge success comes from an enemy continously competing with you, that you have to be alert`

So always want other people to be better than yourself. This is a form of charisma because you really care about them.

`there is no such thing as high paying job, but not hardwork. Maybe there is, but it definitely will not be sustainable`

`Appreciate yourself and your journey`

`You have to create opportunties yourself out of countless failures. Why should people give you? Why should your parents be rich? So go and create yourself.`
